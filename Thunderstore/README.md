# This mod was made by Rusty for the JamesJonesTV Server.

This mods includes over 100+ Roman themed building pieces.
These items do not take damage. They can only be destroyed by the hammer.
These items have configs to edit the recipes.

Everyone is welcome to use this mod.

Feel free to join the discord at https://discord.gg/h6cDGkgE8h and build on the server. Just read the rules before logging on.

You can also find other awesome mods that Rusty has made at https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/RustyMods/
You can donate directly to Rusty at https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/mpei

You can see my other mods at: https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/JamesJonesTV/
